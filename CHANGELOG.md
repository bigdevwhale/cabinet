## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- A museum for any collection: halls, cabinets, cards with photos and your own fields, eight ready kinds of collection.
- Things come in through the phone's camera or from the gallery; the background is cut away on the phone.
- «Is it already on my shelf?»: photograph a thing and it is compared with the whole collection. Search by photo in the catalogue.
- Things without a story stay faded until described; series with empty places, a wish list and a swap fund.
- The museum's levels and ranks, the evening round, the exhibition of the week with a poster, visitors, «on this day», a seasonal inventory, the arrivals book.
- Backup, a PDF catalogue, CSV export. No internet permission.
- English and Russian.
